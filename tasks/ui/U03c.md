# U03c — The room on one screen

Scope accepted by the user on 2026-09-30, after their phone check of U03: the room screen fits one phone screen, with
wireframe A of the canvas `https://claude.ai/artifact/7gP8uzf2QpTr7UvJzTKPc7` ("the diorama") as the target and a pinch
out to see the apartment's plan. Found on the phone: the picture and the top bar take the screen, the room's words fall
below the fold, Go forward is a full-width button of its own, Go back scrolls away and room 1 has none.
Not here: the game's rules, how the room is drawn (U03b), the street, tower and corridor pictures, U04's trace.

## The target (the user's, 2026-09-30)
A room is a place you stand in, not a plan you read. At 360 × 640, with no scroll: the room, the first line of its
words, its relics (tapped in the picture) and every move. The plan from above is the same picture pulled back.

## Plan (amended after the review, 2026-09-30)

### Departures from the mock (Decision 2; asked and picked, 2026-09-30)
1. **At rest the room fills the picture**; the mock rests on a room at 62 % of the picture's shorter side with the plan
   around it (`roomView`). The plan is a pinch out.
2. **The zoom reaches further in** than the mock's `aptLimits` (fit × 1.2 or 280 px a unit): far enough for the
   smallest room of 48 to fill the picture.
3. **The moves sit in the dock on the room screen**, beside the way out; the mock lists them under the picture.
4. **The picture is shorter** than the mock's phone stage (4 : 5, up to 66 % of the screen), and the room's words come
   right under it, before the relic tiles.

### Where wireframe A is not followed (my calls, named)
- **No greyed Leave in rooms 2 and on**: that the way out lies in room 1 is the engine's rule, and the screen would
  re-derive it. The dock shows what is offered.
- **The name stays over the picture** (U01b's caption, every drawn place alike); A put it under.
- **The words are not clipped with a "More ›"**: a room's words are two or three sentences; they show whole.
- **Back into the room is a pinch in** (or any move); no tap on your room on the whole plan — it would sit under the
  relics' hits, which lie there at every scale, and take one.

### 1. The picture (`src/ui/scene/`)
- `PlanCamera.room(index)` (`PlanCamera.ts:72-81`): the room's box, walls included, fitted to the picture on both axes
  with a small margin, in place of `ROOM_SHARE`. The top of the zoom range (`clamp`, `TIGHT`) never stops short of a
  room's rest (departure 2).
- **A pinch settles** (the pick below): let go after a pinch, the view glides to the room you stand in or to the whole
  plan, whichever scale is nearer by ratio (`PlanCamera.settle(framing, rest)`). A drag still coasts and stays where it
  lands. The gesture says how it lets go: `PlanGesture.release({ camera, framing, rest, now, still, ride, coast })`
  returns the motion, a `PlanGlide` — `PlanDrag` the coast (its own speed, 620 ms, the coast easing; none when
  `still`), `Pinch` the settle (the camera's `pace`, the ride easing). `PlanScene.#up` (`PlanScene.ts:341-351`) runs
  what it gets and never asks which gesture it was; `speed()` leaves the interface.
- **The corner map** shows, as today, whenever the plan runs past the frame (`overflows`): at the rest of every room of
  an apartment of 2 rooms or more, at the measured picture sizes (unit-tested, not assumed); a one-room apartment has
  none — the room is the whole plan. **A tap on it pulls back to the whole plan** (the pick below), in place of gliding
  to the spot tapped (`PlanScene.ts:363-366`); `MinimapView.framingAt` goes from the interface, `Minimap` and
  `NoMinimap`; the comments that say "goes there" are fixed (`MinimapView.ts`, `PlanScene.ts:24, 40, 353`,
  `PlanGlide.ts:6`). **An option's hit wins over the corner map** (`#tap` asks the hits first), so a doorway in the
  room's top wall under the map still takes its tap.
- **Reduced motion** (Decision 4): the settle and the pull back jump at once.

### 2. The screen (`src/ui/screens/`, `app.css`)
- **Moves in the dock**, decided by the drawing so the presenter never asks the kind: `Drawing.arrange(parts)` with
  `parts = { moves, leave, system }` (the presenter's `OptionVM`s) returns a `DockLayout` (plain view-model data:
  `strip` — the moves under the picture; `row` — the buttons always out, each `{ option, role }`, role `out` or
  `move`; `fold` — the rest behind More). Each `Drawing` member holds a `MovesPlace` and hands on to it:
  `MovesInDock` (`DrawnPlan`) puts the moves in the row after the way out; `MovesInStrip` (every other member) keeps
  today's strip. `HudVM`'s `moves`, `dock` and `fold.after` become the layout's; `options` still lists every button
  once. `HudPresenter.ts:94-98, 187-192` hand it the parts.
- **The dock's row** (`app.css:1527-1546, 1625-1640`): the way out's look (yellow, wider) moves from `:first-child` to
  the role the view writes (`data-role="out"`). A middle room's `GO BACK · GO FORWARD · MORE` fits one row at 360 px;
  room 1's `▲ LEAVE THE APARTMENT` does not share a row with two more, so there the way out takes the row's full width
  and `GO FORWARD · MORE` share the next — measured in the build, judged by the first-screen check below.
- **The dock's region name** "Leave and game" (`HudPresenter.ts:216`) becomes false in a room: it becomes "Actions",
  true on every screen.
- **The order on a room** (`app.css:1669-1706`; the hook stays `.world[data-drawn]:has(.objects)`): the name, the
  picture, the words, the relic tiles, then the chips, furniture and status. The room's `.cap > .body` gives up its
  box (`display: contents`, as `.cap` and `.side` already do) so `.desc` can be ordered before the tiles; its parts keep
  their own spacing. The picture's height is measured again at 360 × 640 and at the device size so the words' first
  line clears the dock; about 38 % of a tall phone at most.
- **The top bar, thinner on every drawn place** (the pick below), CSS only — the markup stays, so the lookups of
  `.hud`, `.stats dd`, `.crumb.you` and the path's count survive (`world.spec.ts:56, 187`, `scene.spec.ts:62`,
  `ritual.spec.ts:185`, `buildings.spec.ts:36, 91`, `announce.spec.ts:72`): the meter, its percentage, Steps and
  Buffer on one row; the game's name kept for readers with the `.vh` class (never `display: none`, which
  `a11y.spec.ts:24-43` would read as no h1); the depth rail's separators go on a phone so its glyphs sit on one row,
  wrapping only when the trail is longer than the width (deep below the bedrock). No scrolling, so the Shell's is the
  only one.

### Picks for the user's go (each named, changeable)
- **A pinch settles** on the room or the plan. The other way: a pinch stays where it is let go, as today.
- **View controls in a picture** (the open question from U03): a tap that moves the view and picks nothing — the
  corner map pulling back — is allowed, and `web/CLAUDE.md`'s wall says so. The other way: the pinch alone, the corner
  map's tap as today.
- **The thinner top bar on every drawn place** (street, building, corridor, room): the bar is the same element on each,
  and a room-only shape would jump as you walk in. The other way: the room only.

### Shape table
| what | kind | owner | the one fact it owns | statics + why |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `MovesPlace` | interface | each `Drawing` member | where a place's moves sit: `arrange(parts): DockLayout` | none |
| `MovesInStrip` | value object | every `Drawing` member but `DrawnPlan` | the moves under the picture | none |
| `MovesInDock` | value object | `DrawnPlan` | the moves in the dock's row, after the way out | none |
| `DockLayout` | VM (plain data) | `Drawing.arrange` | the strip, the row with each button's role, the fold | — |

`PlanCamera.settle`, `PlanGesture.release` and `Drawing.arrange` are methods on existing types. OO checks: no
`static`; no branch on a kind — the gesture says how it lets go, the drawing where its moves sit, the view writes a
role the stylesheet reads.
