# U01 — The stage (split: U01a the frame, built; U01b the stage and the street, next)

**Why split (measured before the plan):** at 360 × 640 the street's first row ended at y = 496 with the sticky dock
from 568: 72 px of slack, too little for a drawn street. So the frame shrinks first (U01a); U01b gets the space it frees.

## U01a — plan (as run)

The frame of every world screen; what each place shows (its list, readings, tiles, telemetry, the scan / map / trace
panels, its `.diag` line and its fact labels) stays until that place's iteration. Decision 1's "HUD" is read as the
header: `[SYSTEM_TELEMETRY]` and the diagnostic lines (`SYNC: [STABLE]`, `INFUSION_ACTIVE`) go with their places — the
street in U01b, indoors in U02–U04, the rest in U05/U08.

- **Top** (`section.hud`, frame-coloured border kept): the game's name (the h1, moved into the box), the meter labelled
  `Coherence` / `Integrity`, then `Steps n` and `Buffer n/16`. `role=meter`, `meter`, `coherence`, `band` test ids kept.
- **Depth rail** (`nav.rail > ol[data-testid=path]`): an `li` per level — glyph, kind (read out), name
  (`aria-current` on the last). A phone shows the glyphs and reads the names; a desktop has a 160 px left column with
  the names, sticky. Levels are list items, not buttons (the trace column is U06); TRACE stays in the dock; no `title=`.
- **Card** (`.cap`, `data-rest`, h2): eyebrow in capitals (the 89 `place-kind` pins untouched); the **name as the world
  spells it**; a chip row — the position (`Strata 2 of 20`, the kind's label with a capital) then the facts, each value
  with a first capital (`Baroque`, `[STABLE]` unchanged); prose, `.diag`, status and the moves strip unchanged.
- **Dock:** unchanged, still sticky (so `fold.spec.ts:57-61` still measures it).
- **Retired:** PULSE_TRAVERSAL → Steps; TRACE_BUFFER → Buffer; HOP_DENSITY / ABYSSAL_DEPTH → the rail's length;
  `ORBIT 02/05` → the position chip; LOCUS / VOID_LOCUS, LOCUS_HASH / VOID_HASH and SEED leave the world screen; the
  readout fold (`readout`, `HudVM.readout`, `crumb.tail`) goes. `PlaceSummary.hash`, `.depth`, `Location.hash()` and
  the Null Reach's `0x0000 / UNKNOWN` go with their last reader.

## Choices made in the build (not in the plan)

- **The position chip is `li.chip.pos`, not a `.tag`**, so the specs that count `.tag` (buildings:97, richness:48, :172)
  keep their numbers.
- **The name moved into the HUD box** (`h1.brand`); `.bar` is gone from the world screen. On a phone the box is three
  short lines (name; meter; Steps and Buffer): the name and the stats need about 336 px on one line, and there are 286.
- **The rail wraps rather than scrolling sideways**: 8 levels fit one line at 360 px; a room (13) takes two. No script
  sets a scroll position.
- **The desktop rail is 160 px, not 200**: at 200 the middle column made the scan panel taller than the window, and the
  spotlight left its top 0.5 px above the edge (`fold.spec` scan, desktop, red). Also added `html { scroll-padding-top:
  8px }`: a spotlit panel taller than the window now lands 8 px clear of the top (was flush, by rounding).
- **The seed is on the title screen only**: the plan said "title and recap", but the recap never showed it.
  `survival.spec` now reads the seed from the save after REBUILD.
- **The card is tighter on the world screen** (`.world .cap` padding 10 × 12, gap 6; chips 11.5 px) to win back the
  chips' borders.
- **The Buffer value is unpadded** (`1/16`, was `01/16`).
- **The players guide's `INDEX elsewhere`** named a label no kind has; the new text names the chips as they appear.

## U01b — the stage and the street (outline; planned in full next)

- **The engine hands over:**
  - `PlaceSummary.scene` — a drawing key the place answers (`Location.scene()`, the kind's key by default; a floor will
    answer by its state);
  - `GameOption.address` on travel options — the zoom anchor. It cannot come from the option id: a building lists its
    top floor first;
  - `figure` `{ floors, doors }` on travel options (`Location.figure()`, the building answers);
  - `PlayerSummary.decay` 0…1 — the tear strength, owned by `Coherence` (the band edges live there);
  - `PlaceSummary.noise` — the frame seed's text, from `FrameEntropy`;
  - the street's facts become plain (`Era`, `Culture`) and its `.diag` goes.
- **The UI:**
  - `MotionClock` behind a `FrameSource` interface; the browser adapter is built in `main.ts`, so the clock is testable
    in Node (the unit tests have no DOM);
  - `Tween`;
  - `SceneView`: a pixel budget of about 1.3 MP, dpr ≤ 2; a tap becomes a bubbling `pick` event that `InputRouter`
    checks against what is on offer; the list and the picture light each other through `data-lit`;
  - `SceneRegistry` (key → picture), built in `main.ts` with `street` only;
  - `StreetPicture`: `layout` for the hits and anchors, `paint` for the drawing;
  - the zoom in and out;
  - `CoherenceFx`: its pattern comes from the noise seed; only its animation reads the clock;
  - the name's flicker, in CSS;
  - `CanvasView` moves onto the clock.
- **Unknowns:**
  - laying out 4–20 buildings in 328 px (seed 0000 has 20): two rows facing across the way, slots about 32 px; the list
    twin keeps the 44 px rule;
  - the building has no picture until U02, so a picture tap plays the dive before the step; a list tap or a key enters
    at once.
