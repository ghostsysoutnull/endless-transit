## Plan (amended after the grill, 2026-09-30)

### Departures from the mock (Decision 2) and picks — each asked with the plan
1. **A live glyph a level, as the mock draws them** (`transit-reframed.html:1122-1142`): thirteen small line drawings,
   one a kind, and one more for the null reach (a dark ring); a Layer, the Artery, a Crypt and a Shard take the floor's,
   corridor's, apartment's and room's glyph in the void's red. The glyphs move on the clock, your plate pulsing; under
   reduced motion all hold still. (The band's own picture, shrunk, was weighed and dropped: shrunk, its names fall below 12 px.)
2. **The ships lane is empty berths** (Decision 15): a dashed berth frame at the planet (its orbit) and at the city (its
   berth), in the ships' amber, with no ship in them and no label. The hull diamond, the tow frame and the tether are
   drawn when CONCEPT-001 gives a ship to draw — code with no ship behind it would be a drawing no one calls.
3. **Text at 12 px** (the picture law): the mock writes the kind line, the tags, the lane heads and the drift current's
   words at 8–10 px (`:1161, 1179, 1201-1205`). At 12 px on a 360 px phone: the kind and the scale share the line above
   the name (`PLANET · 10⁷ m` — the ruler is that column of scales; the mock drops its left ruler when small, `:1160`);
   the tags go on the name's line after the name when they fit, else under it; a name too long ends in `…` (its button
   carries it whole).
4. **Each level is a real button** over its row (the pole's accessible twin, Touch first), named
   `Level 04, Planet: Auraea — era Future, culture Baroque, drift Analog · Monolith` (`Level`, not the bands' `Depth`,
   and only one of the two views is in the page at a time); a tap flips the switch to Column and centres that band (the
   wireframe's step 5, `pole-wireframe.html:160`). The canvas is decorative; no hit-test in it.
5. **The default is Column** until the player picks Pole (the mock's default, `:1216`); the choice survives a reload.
   **The rail** (Decision 6) opens the remembered view at the level nearest the finger: its band centred in Column, its
   row centred in Pole.
6. **The vibe's words on the cards** (the mock's chips, `:632-643`): the planet gains `Drift Analog · Monolith`; the
   country its stability; a rebel city's `Rebel district` stays and gains its swapped `Era` and `Culture`; the
   apartment's `TEMPORAL_MARKER` / `TEMPORAL_ANOMALY_DETECTED` become `Era`, `Culture`, `Drift era · culture` naming what
   drifted (`Drift culture`, `Drift era`, or both) and `Temporal anomaly`; a Crypt's `ABYSSAL_RESONANCE DETECTED`
   becomes the apartment's chips plus `Abyssal resonance`. The apartment and the Crypt are seen only as bands. The
   room's chips stay.
7. **Drift is per value** (the game wins on a rule; Decision 14 says "the main pair … else the second"): an apartment
   draws its culture and its era apart (`ApartmentFactory.ts:69-70`), so it may drift in one, the other or both; the
   hook comes in only on the ribbon that drifted. A room carries its apartment's values and does not hook again.
8. **An `anomaly` tag** (not in the mock's tags, `:1113`, nor Decision 13): the one apartment in a hundred out of time
   is a vibe fact of its level, as a drift is; the pole shows it beside the drift tag.

### Engine (`src/engine/`)
1. **`VibeFigure`** (plain data, a union on `held`), names being the lists' keys:
   - `{ held: 'none' }` — above the planet;
   - `{ held: 'planet', era, culture, secondEra, secondCulture, stability }` — the planet, no trait yet;
   - `{ held: 'country', era, culture, secondEra, secondCulture, stability, trait, rebel, drift: { era, culture } }` —
     the country and everything under it.
   `Location.vibeFigure()` reads `this.vibe()`, rebel and drift false; **`City`** answers `rebel` when it carries its
   own swapped vibe; **`Apartment`** answers its own era and culture and drifts where each differs from the vibe's
   main (an anomaly takes the main pair, so it never drifts); **`Room`** answers its apartment's pair, no drift. The
   Artery's and a Crypt's come from the Artery's own vibe (one culture, never drifting).
2. **`PoleSign`** (plain data, a union on `look`): `{ look: 'curved' }` (a corridor whose shape is curved),
   `{ look: 'door', state }` (an apartment whose door is not stable), `{ look: 'anomaly' }`. `Location.poleSigns()`
   answers `[]`; `Corridor` and `Apartment` answer theirs. Rebel and drift are not signs: the figure holds them.
   **Known smell:** "not stable" asks the door's state by its display name, which extends HK-027; logged there.
3. **`GlyphLook`** (a union of the fourteen glyphs' keys) on `LocationKind`, beside its icon and scale; the Layers name
   their twins' glyph and are drawn abyssal by the step's `abyssal`.
4. **The trace step carries them**: `TraceSummary` steps gain `vibe`, `signs` and `glyph` (`GameEngine.ts:248-269`).
5. **Words** (departure 6): `Planet`, `Country`, `City`, `Apartment` and `Crypt` facts. The stability's words have one
   owner, **`Vibe.stabilityText()`** (`79.40%`, the format `ElevatorState.ts:81` writes today), which `ElevatorState`
   and `Country` both ask.

### UI (`src/ui/`)
6. **`PoleVM`** from the HUD presenter, beside the bands in `TraceColumnVM`: the switch's words (`Pole`, `Column`, the
   group's name), the lane heads (`Era`, `Culture`, `Trait`), the drift current's words (`drift · analog`), and a level
   a row — key, glyph, abyssal, kind and scale line, name, button name, `here`, the three values, the second pair,
   rebel, drift, and its tags. Each tag's word and look come from one table in the presenter keyed by the sign's `look`
   and by `rebel` / `drift` / `anomaly` — the picture paints a tag's look and never branches on its kind.
7. **`PoleLayout`** (value object, pure): from the levels and a width, where each row stands (≈ 76 px apart, Decision
   13), the ribbons as runs (a run a value on each lane, a break where a level changes it, and at a rebel level on both
   lanes), the drift current's start, each hook, and each level button's box. The one place the pole's geometry is
   decided; the tests read it.
8. **`PolePicture`** (service, pure): paints a `PoleLayout` at a moment — the lanes, the ribbons with their values along
   the run, the rebel break in red, the drift current dashed with its hooks, the spine with its pulse, the plates with
   their glyphs, the names, tags and the empty berths; reduced motion draws it still.
9. **`PoleGlyph`** (interface, fourteen classes, `Record<GlyphLook, PoleGlyph>` built in `ScenePictures`): a kind's
   small live drawing at a radius and a moment, the mock's `MINI`.
10. **`TracePole`** (entity, in `src/ui/scene/`, built in `main.ts` and handed to the view beside `TraceBands`): mounts
    the pole's canvas, runs the pulse and the glyphs on the clock while the pole shows, and keeps the level asked for
    in view (you, or the rail's).
11. **`TraceView`** (`'pole' | 'column'`) and **`TraceViewMemory`** (interface owned by the view: `recall()`,
    `remember(view)`), answered by **`LocalStorageTraceViewMemory`** in `src/platform/`: a getter, as the save store
    takes it; what it reads is `unknown` until one function turns it into a `TraceView` — anything but `pole` recalls
    Column — and a blocked store forgets quietly.
12. **`HudView`'s template**: the switch in the column's header (two buttons, `aria-pressed`, view controls with no
    `data-option`), the column's body holding either the bands or the pole with its level buttons.
