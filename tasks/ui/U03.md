# U03 — The apartment and the room

Scope accepted by the user on 2026-09-29 (Solo loop): the apartment's floor plan and the room drawn in it, on the room
screen; the room's own words plain. Not here: the street, the tower, the corridor's picture (U01b, U02), the Layers

## Plan (amended after the grill, 2026-09-29)

### How the game maps onto the mock (the user's pick, 2026-09-29: one picture that zooms)
The mock stands you in the apartment and lets you tap any room. The game has no such stop: a door drops you into room
1, the rooms are walked strictly in order (`Room.ts` MOVES: back, forward), and only room 1 has the way out
(`Room.exit`). So there is **one picture on the room screen, the plan, resting zoomed into the room you stand in**:
- **At rest** your room is framed as the mock frames a room it glides to (`roomView`, `transit-reframed.html:615`),
  drawn in full inside its walls — back wall, floor, light, furniture, the relics you tap; its neighbours peek in at
  the edges, the doorways to them in its walls.
- **Drag** pans one to one and coasts; **pinch** zooms within the mock's limits (`aptLimits`, `:610`: fit × 0.85 to
  max(fit × 1.2, 280 px a unit)). Rooms not yet reached are in fog. A **minimap** shows the whole plan and the view
  when the plan overflows the frame; a tap on it glides there — a view control like the drag, not an option.
- **Tap a doorway** (or Go back / Go forward): the view glides through into that room, then the move runs; the new room
  is already framed when it arrives. Other rooms are drawn, never tappable (the game walks in order).
- **Tap a relic** (or its tile): the take runs; the relic **flies to the HUD's Buffer count** (departure 4).
- **Leave** (room 1 only: the dock's button, or the entrance in the picture): the view pulls back to the whole plan,
  then the leave runs; the corridor then zooms out of the door (U02's `arrive`, unchanged).
- **Arriving from the corridor** (a new scene): the plan shows whole, then glides into room 1 — going in zooms in.
- **What you point at lights its twin**: a relic and its tile, a doorway and its move button, the entrance and the
  leave (Decision 1).
- Reduced motion: no coast, glide, pull back or flight; the view jumps, a tap runs at once.
- A Crypt's Shards (below the bedrock) are rooms: the same picture, drawn by their own keys (`abyssal`).

### Departures from the mock (Decision 2)
1. **Rooms chain, not branch — the layout** (asked of the user with this plan). The mock's plan is a balanced tree of
   splits, doors wherever two halves meet, rooms numbered breadth-first. The game walks room *n* to *n + 1*, so each
   must share a wall with the next. A balanced split cannot promise that; the layout **peels one room at a time**: the
   rest of the footprint is always entered along one whole side, which the room just peeled covers; the next room is
   a strip cut off that rest, across its longer side (rooms stay squarish), at either end (by hash), sized by its
   share of the area (± 20 %). The last room is what remains. Invariant: room *n + 1* touches room *n* along ≥ one
   door's width. The look: a spiral of strips and blocks rather than the mock's even halves.
2. **No stop in the apartment**: the user's pick above.
3. **Text at 12 px**, as in U02 (the mock draws 9–10 px): a room's name on the plan only when its box is ≥ 90 px wide,
   else its number; relic names two words at most, the rest on the tile below.
4. **The flight lands on the HUD's Buffer count**, not a Buffer button: on a phone the dock's Buffer is folded behind
   MORE (`HudPresenter.ts:180`).

### Engine (`src/engine/`)
1. **`PlanPortrait`** (a `Portrait` member) → `PortraitReader.plan(plan: PlanFigure)`. `Location.portrait(seen)` gains
   the predicate `scan(seen)` already takes, passed from `GameEngine.#summaryOf`; `Room` reads it, and the other
   overrides (`Street`, `Building`, `Floor`, `Layer`) keep taking none — an override may accept fewer parameters.
   Readers answering `plan`: `SceneDrawing`, `FloorPad`, `tests/support/readPortrait.ts`.
2. **`PlanFigure`** (plain data): `rooms` — asked of the apartment (`Apartment.plan(seen)`), per room (`PlanRoom`) its
   `address`, `name`, `sight` (`RoomSight`: `visited` | `known` | `fog` — visited; led to from a visited room; or any,
   once surveyed — **the engine owns fog**, asking each room where its moves lead) and `relics` (the relic marks to
   draw: what lies there when visited or surveyed, else 0); `here`, the address of the room you stand in; `look`,
   its `RoomLook`.
3. **`RoomLook`** (value object, owned by `Room`): the keys the room's words were drawn from — `Atmosphere.keys =
   { walls, light }` (a culture key and an era key; after a glitch's swap the swapped key; after `#pool`'s fallback
   the fallback key, the one the words actually came from), `cold` (below 8 °C, the mock's line), the furniture count,
   `anomaly`. `RoomContents.test:186-194` compares a whole `Atmosphere` — re-pinned.
4. **The survey** (pick): `Location.survey()` — a command, no-op by default; `Room.survey()` tells its apartment;
   `Apartment` keeps `#surveyed`, remembers `{"surveyed":true}`, recalls only that shape. The scan command calls
   `here.survey()` after its report; `scan()` stays a query. `keystone-and-descent.json` scans in a room, so
   `Sessions.test` replays a surveyed save.
5. **Move options carry where they lead**, through a query: `MoveTable.to(self, id)` (the rule's `to`, never `act` —
   `make` runs `act`, and the elevator's `corridor` rule enters the corridor) behind `Location.leadsTo(id)`;
   `#moveOptions` fills `address` and `place`. `GameEngine.test.ts:398-405, 475-477` compare whole move options on a
   floor and a corridor — re-pinned.
6. **Words** (U03b; Decision 1, the prose stays): position `Room 1 of 4` (index label `CELL` → `Room`); chips `Era
   Future`, `Type Neural link array`, `Oxygen 20%`, `Temp 23°C`, `Signal Clear`, `Degraded` (alert) only under an
   anomaly; no status line (it repeats the chips); leave `Leave the apartment` / `Leave the crypt`; the aside
   `Furniture: …` and `Relics 6`; the full note `The buffer is full: merge or drop a fragment to take more.`; a door's
   name `Brutalist Slab, polished` (the word goes from the name: it is drawn on the door and kept in its appearance);
   `APPEARANCE` goes from its reading's label; `HelpPresenter.ts:59` says the new leave.

### UI (`src/ui/`)
7. **`PlanLayout`** (service, pure): departure 1's peel, from the room count (1–48) and the address (`SceneHash`):
   rooms as boxes in plan units, the doors between *n* and *n + 1* (centred on their shared span), the entrance on room
   1's bottom wall. Property test, 1–48 rooms × 200 addresses: the boxes tile the footprint; each pair shares ≥ one
   door's span; room 1 touches the bottom; no room thinner than a third of its length; deterministic.
8. **`Framing`** (value object): centre and scale; `between(other, t)`, `equals`. **`PlanCamera`** (value object, the
   picture's answer at a size): fit, the range, `clamp` (the mock's `clampAc`), `rest(here)` (`roomView`), `whole()`,
   a glide's pace, a coast's landing.
9. **`Pinch`** (value object): two pointers' start and now → the framing (the mock's pinch about the midpoint).
   `Fling` is reused per axis.
10. **`PlanPicture`** (service, pure; the mock's `apartment` and `room`), behind its own interface
    **`PlanDrawing<PlanVM>`** (`layout(vm, size, framing)`, `paint(…, framing, …)`, `camera(vm, size)`), since
    `ScenePicture` takes a number: hits of type **`PlanHit`** (`SceneHit` for the doorways, entrance and relics by
    option id, each ≥ 44 px; the minimap as its own box, no id); paint: the grid, walls, rooms by `sight`, here and
    lit, doors, the entrance, numbers and names, relic marks, the visited dot, you; the room you stand in in full once
    its box is large (U03a plain; U03b the mock's `room`: wall pattern by `keys.walls`, light by `keys.light`, snow
    when cold, furniture, relics lit and labelled; an unknown key plain); the minimap.
11. **Hosts.** `SceneView`'s view is one number and its camera has 14 one-number members; the plan's view is a
    `Framing`. **`PlanScene`** hosts it: pan, pinch, coast, **`PlanTrip`** (a glide, a pull back or the arrival, then
    its pick, if any), the minimap, reduced motion, the twin lighting; `arrive(id)` is a no-op (the view is already
    framed; `HudView`'s `Retrace` calls it with the move just undone after every step). What both hosts share leaves
    `SceneView` first, with no behaviour change: **`SceneCanvas`** (mount the canvas, wire its listeners, paint under
    the zoom and the tear), made by a **`SceneCanvasMaker`** built in `main.ts` and handed to both hosts.
12. **Sketch split and the stage.** `Sketch` today carries one-number calls, so it splits: **`Sketch`** (what the
    screen holds: `frame`, `drawn`, `samePicture`, `stageOn(stage: SceneStages)`) and **`LineSketch`** (today's
    number calls; `Sketched` implements it). The plan's is **`Planned`** (`PlanSketch`: `PlanDrawing` + `PlanVM`).
    **`SceneStages`** (the port `stageOn` receives: `line(sketch: LineSketch)`, `plan(sketch: PlanSketch)`) is
    implemented by **`SceneStage`** (entity: the host shown now; keeps it while the picture stays, else disposes it and
    makes the right one through **`SceneHosts`** — `line(onLight)`, `plan(onLight)` — the makers `SceneViewMaker` and
    **`PlanSceneMaker`** built in `main.ts`). Each host takes its own sketch type (no cast, no narrowed override);
    `DrawnScene` loses `View<Sketch>` for the calls the stage needs. `SceneStage` is built in `main.ts` and handed to
    `HudView`. `DrawnPlan` (the `Drawing` member) and `PictureBook.plan(vm)` / `SceneRegistry.plan`.
13. **`SceneDrawing`**: `Drawings.of(place, options, decay)` takes the options split by role (`{ travel, moves,
    leave, takes }`) — `SceneDrawing.test` and the presenter fakes move. `plan` → `DrawnPlan`: the doorway moves
    joined to rooms by address, the leave, the takes by ordinal (relic *i* is take *i*, `GameEngine.#takeOptions`).

14. **`RelicFlight`** (U03b; service, built in `main.ts`, handed to `PlanScene`): a mark from the relic to the Buffer
    stat (`data-testid="stat-buffer"`, added), about 650 ms, then the stat bumps; none under reduced motion.
15. **First screen**: on a drawn room the relic tiles come before the body (a one-room apartment has no move; its first

16. **Docs**: the player guide and cheat sheet (citations re-read), `web/CLAUDE.md` ("a drawn place", the hosts),
