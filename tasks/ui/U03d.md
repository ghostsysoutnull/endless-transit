# U03d — The room, large

Asked by the user on 2026-09-30 after U03c: the room shown much larger, the corner map behind a key and off by default,
easy reach of the room's words and relics (a scroll is fine), and the buffer's limit of sixteen removed. Built in a fast
loop — each try published for the user's phone check with the tests left for after (the user's call) — then hardened.

## Picks (the user's, 2026-09-30)
- **The room is its own view** (option A): standing in a room, its inside fills the whole picture, every room the same
  size whatever its box on the plan. The plan shows only on the MAP key, and a new room always starts inside. (A first
  try that only made the picture taller was not enough: a wide room in a tall picture still filled only its width.)
- **The words a scroll away**: a button under the picture, `About this room · N relics ↓`, jumps to them.
- **No limit on the buffer**: a separate commit; a take is never sealed, a save of any size is taken.
- **Hardened before U04** (option A of "what now").

## Departures from the mock (Decision 2)
The mock rests on a room at 62 % of the picture with the plan around it; here the room fills the picture, and the plan
is a key away rather than a pinch. Asked and picked above.

## As built
- The view: `Framing` gains a vertical stretch; `PlanCamera.inside(index)` stretches a room's box to the picture but for
  the margin. `PlanScene` holds a `ViewMode` — `InRoom` (still, `inside` framings, no corner map) or `OverPlan` (U03c's
  plan: pan, pinch, settle, the corner map) — and a MAP key over the picture that flips them; its words ride in the
  `PlanVM`. A doorway glides from room to room, both filling the picture.
- The screen: the room's picture runs down to the peek button above a one-row dock (`100dvh - 264px`); room 1's way out
  shares the dock's row, its words on two lines.
- The buffer: `Buffer` holds any number; `full()` and `capacity()` are gone with the sealed takes, the full note and the
  save's size check; the HUD and the buffer screen count without a total.

### Tests
Unit: the room standing in it fills the picture at both measured sizes for 1–48 rooms; the stretch maps and pans
true; doorways and relics lie in the picture from inside a middle room; the drawing's stops from inside — each shown
red on a broken build. The sixteen's tests deleted or turned (a buffer past sixteen, takes open past it, a save of
forty). The goldens' `Buffer n/16` lines rewritten once, the diff read. Browser: the room's first screen (the picture
at least 55 % of the screen, every move and the way to the words above the dock), the MAP key's flip and a new room
starting inside (shown red with the reset removed), the plan's pinch and corner map behind the key, reduced motion, a
picture that changes height keeping the room filling it (shown red before its fix), a buffer of twenty on a phone.

### The design check (once, about 104k)
Two fixes: where an option leads was decided twice (`stopOf` and `inside`, `rest` and `home`) — now one `stopOf` and one
`rest`, each told how a room is framed (`RoomFrame`, which each `ViewMode` is); a finger landing on a flip's glide left
the plan stretched — fingers now wait for it, and `landing` keeps the stretch. It also pointed at a resize keeping the
old framing, which a phone's address bar would show as a room no longer filling the picture: fixed (`ViewMode.refit`),
its browser test red first. The map key's words got one type (`MapKey`). Seven tidying findings logged as HK-036.
