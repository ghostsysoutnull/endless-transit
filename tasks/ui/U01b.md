# U01b — The street, drawn

Plan approved by the user on 2026-09-25, with **amendment A**: on a phone the name comes first, then the picture, then
the list, then the rest of the card. The build followed it. Where it departs from the plan, the "Choices" section says so.

## What was built
- **Engine.** `Location.drawing()` returns the kind's key and reaches the screen as `PlaceSummary.drawing`.
  `PlaceSummary.noise` is the text of the frame seed.
  - `Location.figure()` returns `{ floors, doors }` for a `Building` and nothing for any other kind.
  - Every option now carries `address` and `figure`. Only a travel option fills them; the others get `''` and `null`.
  - `Coherence.decay()` is 0 from 70 up and rises to 1 at 0. The 70 comes from `BANDS`.
  - The street's facts are labelled `Era` and `Culture`, and its `status()` returns `''`.
- **UI, in `src/ui/scene/`.**
  - Timing: `FrameSource`, `MotionClock` (one loop, it runs only while something listens, and it calls a snapshot of its
    listeners), `Tween` and `PixelBudget`.
  - Drawing: `ScenePicture`, `SceneHit` (the hit box plus the zoom anchor), `SceneRegistry`, `StreetPicture` and
    `CoherenceFx` with its `FxPlan`. `SceneEvents` names the `pick` and `light` events.
  - `SceneView` hosts the canvas. `SceneVM` travels in `HudVM.drawing`.
  - Elsewhere: `platform/BrowserFrameSource.ts`, and `canvas/StylePalette.ts`, which `CanvasView` and `SceneView`
    share.
- **Wiring.**
  - `main.ts` builds the clock and the registry (`street` only).
  - `HudView(clock, scenes)` renders the scene host only when the registry has a picture for the key. It keeps the lit
    id and the last address. On a street it zooms out of the child whose address is a segment prefix of the last
    address.
  - `InputRouter` listens for `pick`.
  - `CanvasView` now runs on the clock and keeps the budget. Its own rAF loop and `MAX_DPR` are gone.
- **CSS.**
  - `.cap` is split into `.head` and `.body`.
  - `.scene` is a relative box; the canvas is absolute with `inset: 0`, so its size never feeds back into the layout.
  - Desktop: a `[data-drawn]` grid puts the picture above the card, full width, 4:3, capped at 60vh.
  - Phone, amendment A: `.cap` and `.side` become `display: contents`, and the parts are ordered hud, rail, head,
    scene, travel, body, aside, panels, dock.
  - The name flickers in the degraded and critical bands, only under `no-preference`.
  - A lit row gets `.row[data-lit]`.

The OO rules hold:
- There is no `static` and no `instanceof` on a place or an option. `instanceof` appears only on DOM events.
- Nothing switches on a kind: the drawing key is a registry lookup.
- Adapters are built only in `main.ts`.

## Choices the build made (in the plan's spirit, or departing from it)
- **The phone picture is 277 px at 360 × 640, not ≈ 290.**
  - It is sized `clamp(200px, 100dvh − 363px, 44dvh)`. The cap is 44dvh, not about 42vh, so that 640 reaches the full
    277.
  - Two things set that height: the worst first row, which wraps to two lines at 61 px, and an 8 px margin.
  - The longest name the lists can make is `The <noun> of <concept>`, 31 characters, and it still wraps to two lines.
  - The longest street name is 16 characters and does not wrap the h2.
- **On a phone the name has no box.** It stands over the picture as its caption, which buys 18 px. The rest of the card
  keeps the card box.
- **`display: contents` on the card on a drawn phone layout.** `[data-rest]` cannot take the focus there.
  - The Shell never rests the focus on the street: the street has no moves, and its rows are always shown.
  - `focus.spec` is green on the phone.
  - On a phone the pane map comes after the card body, before the panels.
- **`HudVM.drawing` is always present.** A `SceneVM` carries the place's address, so the zoom out works. The view
  decides by registry lookup whether to draw it (`data-drawn`).
- **The name flicker is only on drawn places**, per "every other place is unchanged". It is off under reduced motion
  through a `no-preference` media rule. A rule in the reduced block lost to the flicker selector's specificity; the
  scene spec caught that as one running animation.
- **Sealed buildings keep their hit box**, so they still light, but a tap on one is ignored.
- **A zoom in drifts the anchor to the middle** while scaling 1 → 6 and fading to the ground. It dispatches `pick` only
  when it ends, as the last statement of the frame.
- **A render with the same `SceneVM` object changes nothing**, so MORE, DEBUG and hovers do not cancel a zoom. A new step
  cancels it and drops its pick.
- **A building is never wider than half the height its row can reach.** Without that, the short street's four slots
  drew slabs, which looked wrong in the screenshot.
- **Paint order:** the far row, then the way, then the near row, then the rain. The numbers test compares the set of
  numbers, not their order.
- **`scene.spec` pins how fast a reduced-motion tap enters:** within 250 ms, well inside the 450 ms zoom. On a touch
  screen the click lands a moment after the touch ends. The spec scrolls the scene into view before it sweeps, because
  a reload restores the scroll.

## What the tester can try
- **Open the street** on a phone. The name comes first, then the drawn street: two rows of buildings along the way, rain,
  flickering windows, and numbers under their feet. The list comes next, then the chips (`Era Future`,
  `Culture Baroque`). The `SYNC` line is gone.
- **Enter from the picture:** tap a building to zoom into it and enter it. LEAVE zooms back out of it.
- **Enter from the list:** a row, or its key on a desktop, enters at once.
- **Light the twin:** on a desktop, hover or focus a row to light its building, or hover a building to light its row.
- **Watch it tear:** with `?debug`, pick INTEGRITY 39, 29 or 1. The picture tears, gets grain, a red cast and an
  occasional dark flash, and the name flickers.
- **Turn on reduced motion:** the picture is a still, a tap enters at once, and the tear is drawn but does not move.
