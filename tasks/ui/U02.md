# U02 — Inside the building: the tower ridden, the corridor walked

Scope accepted by the user on 2026-09-26 (Solo loop): the tower on the building screen and on a floor at the elevator,
the corridor on a floor in the corridor mode, both on the street's scene host with the list as twin; the building's,
floor's and corridor's own labels made plain. Not here: the apartment and the room (U03), the Layers' own screens and
the trace (U04), the vibe and the pole (U05). No game rule changes: every tap runs an option the game already offers.

## Plan (amended after the grill, 2026-09-26)

### How the game maps onto the mock (the game wins on rules, Decision 2)
- The mock goes building → corridor; the game goes building → **floor at the elevator** (moves: Up, Down, Enter
  Corridor, Descend) → **floor in the corridor mode** (the doors). So:
  - **building screen**: the tower. Its children (the floors, and the Layers once breached) are the hits and the
    pad. A drag or the gauge rides the car one to one (a view only — the game's elevator moves on arrival, as
    today); a tap on a floor (picture or pad) rides the car there, then enters it — no zoom: the next screen is the
    same tower (the ride is the motion).
  - **floor at the elevator**: the same tower, the car parked at this floor. No children, so no hits, no drag, no
    gauge; the moves stay buttons. Up / Down ride the car one floor (the scene is kept across the step and the car
    travels from the old floor to the new one). LEAVE back to the building: no zoom out (same picture).
  - **floor in the corridor mode**: the corridor, first person. Its children (the doors) are the hits and the list;
    a drag or the scrub slider walks; a tap on a door (picture or list) walks to it, zooms in, then opens it.
    Coming back out of an apartment: the view stands at that door, then zooms out of it.
  - A Layer keeps its old screen (drawing key `layer`, no picture until U04).

### Departures from the mock's look (Decision 2 — asked of the user, 2026-09-26)
- **The pad's keys above its tabs of tens** (the mock: tabs first) — accepted (A). Why: the first screen at
  360 × 640 — a floor key must show above the dock, and two rows of tabs would push the keys under it.
- **Tower rows as the mock draws them, by a peek (user's idea)**: each floor's corridor shape and its doors' material
  and state are read from the seeds when the floor is made — the same deals (`Sentences`, `Doors`) on the same seed
  path (`Progeny`'s child seed, the apartment's `door` branch) — without creating the corridor or the apartments. A
  test pins the peek equal to the made corridor and doors for many seeds. So no departure there.

### The peek (engine)
- `Doors.look(seed)` → `{ material, state }` (the deal `of()` already makes, now one method both use);
  `Sentences.dealtPair(seed)`; `Progeny.childSeed(parentSeed, index)` (the one rule `exactly` uses); the apartment's
  `door` branch key exported from `ApartmentFactory`.
- `FloorFactory.create` hands the floor its `passage`: `{ shape, looks }` for its corridor-to-be (a procgen
  `Passages` service composes the above); a Layer's is empty. `Floor.figure()` → `{ floors: 0, doors, shape, looks }`
  — no child made.
- `Building.portrait()` carries `tower.rows`: each floor's figure (the floors are the building's own children, made
  when the building lists them, as today); a floor at the elevator hands the building's portrait with the car at it.

### Engine (`src/engine/`)
1. **`Figure`** grows optional parts (plain data): `tower?: { address, landmark, car, below }` (the building's address
   and landmark — what its roof is drawn from, the same on the street and inside —, the floor the car stands at, how
   many Layers lie open below the bedrock: 0 until the breach), `shape?: string` (how a corridor runs),
   `door?: { material, state, words }` (a door's material and state as their list keys, lowercase, and the word
   written on it, empty when none).
   - `Location.figure()` stays "this place's shape on its parent's picture" (cheap: never generates children).
     `Apartment.figure()` → `{ floors: 0, doors: 0, door }`; `Corridor.figure()` → `{ floors: 0, doors:
     doorsPerFloor(), shape }` (doors from the building, never `children()`); `Building.figure()` and a floor's stay
     as they are (a floor has none).
   - **`Location.portrait()`** (new, default `figure()`): what the place's own picture needs. `Building.portrait()` →
     `{ floors, doors, tower }`; `Floor.portrait()` asks its state: `ElevatorState` → the building's portrait with
     `car` = this floor (no shape: the corridor is not dealt at the elevator); `CorridorState` → the building's
     portrait plus `shape` from `corridor().figure()` (already dealt in this mode).
   - `PlaceSummary.figure` = `here.portrait()`.
2. **The corridor's shape comes from its words**: `themes/descriptions/corridor.txt` becomes `sentence|shape` pairs
   (`long`, `service`, `curved`, `static`); `Sentences` (the one owner of the deal) gains `dealtPair(seed)` on the
   same `sentence` branch — `Seed.pick` depends on the list length only, so the same line as today. A step-0 test pins
   today's sentence for fixed seeds before the switch; `ContentFloors` pins the four shape keys.
3. **Drawing keys**: `Floor.drawing()` asks its state — `ElevatorState` → `BUILDING_KIND.key()` (the tower),
   `CorridorState` → `CORRIDOR_KIND.key()`; `Layer.drawing()` → `LAYER_KIND.key()`. `FloorState` gains `drawing()`
   and `portrait(floor)`.
4. **`GameOption.numbered`**: the listed place goes by its own number (`child.goesByNumber()`): a screen may lay the
   list out as a pad of numbers. `systemOption` gives `false`.
5. **Plain words** (Decision 1; the room's prose stays):
   - Building: position chip `Building 2 of 20` (indexLabel); facts `Culture`, `Floors 16`, a landmark as the chip
     `Landmark`; the status line plain — none when untouched, `3 merges made inside`, `The bedrock is breached`;
     description `An elevator runs the height of the building.`; heading `Ride to a floor:`; verb `Ride to`
     (`Ride to Floor 5`, `Ride to Lobby`).
   - Floor: no position chip (indexLabel `''`; the engine gives no position for a kind without an index label);
     facts `Era`, `Culture`, `Stability`, `Trait` (`Standard` when none); description the floor's sentence only
     (the `Local signal is STABLE…` line goes); `{culture}` written with a capital (`Void`, not `VOID`); diagnostic
     none (a Layer keeps its own, U04). Readings on the building's list: `Zone` with the zone plain
     (`Hydroponic bay`, from `HYDROPONIC_BAY`, in the engine's reading); `ST 100%` and `RES Hz` go from `Floor`
     (`Layer.readings` keeps its own, and `Floor.resonance()` stays for it).
   - Corridor mode: facts `Culture`, `Doors 9`; status none; heading `Doors:`; verb `Open` (`Open Heavy Bulkhead`).
   - Not touched: the scan tables, the trace's `[FLOORS: n]`, the telemetry pane (shared by every indoor place —
     retired as one piece, U03/U06), door names (the apartment's, U03), `CONDUIT` (never shown).

### UI (`src/ui/`)
6. **`SceneCamera`** (plain data, a picture's answer): where the view rests, its range, how a finger drags it (axis,
   units a pixel, 0 = no drag), the coast and whether it settles on whole numbers, the trip pace
   (`min(most, base + per·√distance)` — the elevator accelerates, cruises and brakes), whether going in zooms
   (`zoom`), the stops (each child's view position, by option id, in slider order) and the slider's box on the
   picture (`null` = no slider). `ScenePicture` gains `camera(vm, size): SceneCamera | null`; `layout`/`paint` take
   the view position (`StreetPicture` answers `null`, ignores it, and keeps zooming).
7. **`Fling`** (value object): samples of (time, value) over the last 110 ms and the speed of a release (0 if the
   finger stopped > 90 ms ago) — the mock's `sample`/`vel`.
8. **`SceneTrip`** (value object): one going-in — the view's `Tween` to the child's stop, then (if the camera zooms)
   the scale's `Tween`, then its pick; answers the view, the scale and whether it is over at a time. Unit-tested with
   fixed times.
9. **`SceneView`** keeps the view position:
   - a new view-model **drops any trip, zoom or coast in flight and its pick** (ids are positional — U01b's rule
     widened); then the same address keeps the view, a changed address in the same picture rides from the old view
     to the new rest (floor → floor), else the view starts at rest;
   - a drag on the canvas (past 6 px) moves the view one to one and coasts on release (settling on a floor for the
     tower); while the tower is dragged the floor under the car is announced (`light`), so the pad's group follows the
     car (the mock's `S.group`); a click after a drag is not a tap; `touch-action: none` on a canvas that drags;
   - a tap, or `enter(id)` from the list, on an open child with a stop runs a `SceneTrip`; taps are ignored while one
     runs;
   - `arrive(id)` (coming back out) puts the view at the child's stop, then zooms out if the camera zooms;
   - the slider: a `div[role=slider]` over the picture at the camera's box (≥ 44 px thick), beside the canvas — the
     canvas carries `role=img` and the picture's name, the host none (a slider inside an image is lost to readers);
     `aria-label` and the value's text from the view-model (the stop child's name); pointer (grab, glide, follow,
     coast) and the arrow keys; drawn by the picture, transparent, with a focus ring;
   - reduced motion: no coast, no trip, no zoom — the view jumps, a tap enters at once.
10. **`TowerPicture`** (key `building`, the mock's `building`): a window of thumb-sized floors (4 at 360 × 640)
    centred on the car, the shaft and the car with its cable, each floor's number, windows and its corridor as a plain
    line with a tick per door (the departure above), lit / visited marks; the roof (the street's roof, shared) when
    the top is in view, `▲ n` / `▼ n` counts, the bedrock as a red hatch (sealed) or broken open with the Layers' rows
    below it (breached); the gauge on the right (track, ticks labelled where 16 px apart, visited marks, the window,
    the car). Camera: view = the car's floor; rest = `tower.car`; min = −below, max = floors − 1; drag on `y`, one
    floor a row; settles on a floor; pace 320 + 230·√d ≤ 2400 ms; no zoom; stops = each child at its number; slider
    = the gauge (none without children).
11. **`CorridorPicture`** (key `corridor`, the mock's `corridor`): first person, the doors in pairs down both walls,
    near ones large, far ones in haze; by shape — `curved` bends away under one strip of light, `service` narrow with
    flush doors to a blank end wall, `long` fading into the dark, `static` ending in moving static; each door's number,
    lit / visited marks, its words when near; its look by **material** (a panel pattern by family: glass, metal,
    stone, timber, bone; any other plain) and by **state** as the mock draws them (frozen: frost; cold: a blue glow;
    static: scan lines; the rest plain); the scrub slider along the bottom (a tick per door by side and state, the
    window, you, the two ends as glyphs). Camera: the mock's `doorZ`/`hallLen`/`camFor`; drag on `y` (swipe up walks
    on, 1/40 per px); coast 0.3 s; pace 260 + 220·√d ≤ 1500 ms; zooms.
12. **Shared**: `SceneHash` (the FNV fraction now private to `StreetPicture`) and `Roof` (which roof a building has,
    from its address and landmark) — one owner each; a step-0 test pins today's numbers (fractions and roof kinds
    for fixed addresses) before the move.
13. **Inks**: `cy` and `bl` join `SURFACE_INKS` (strokes and fills, never text).
14. **`HudPresenter`**: `SceneVM` gains `tower` (floors, doors, address, landmark, car, below; `null` without), `shape`,
    `slider` (the list's heading, the slider's name) and per child `material`, `state`, `words`. A list whose every
    row is `numbered` becomes a **pad**: `HudVM.pad = { label, groups: [{ label '0–9', rows }], open }` — one group up

15. **`HudView`**: renders the pad — the open group's keys (a real `button[data-option]` each, the number shown, its
