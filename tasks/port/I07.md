## Guide-vs-code disagreements (Decision 6) and what the Guide does not say

1. **`Door.visited` never set** (HOUSEKEEPING.md:34, Door.groovy:57): the visited mark is the traveller's footprint
   (one owner, I05) — `[V]` on the row and `[VISITED]` in the scan; nothing else to set.
2. **The memento carried no breach flag** (HK-023): the building's memento has `breached`; a save below the bedrock
   restores because the states are recalled *before* any place is looked for — a breach unseals the Layers the
   save stands on. `Journey.restore` reordered; the Descent tests and the fixture pin it.
3. **The unreachable `[TEMPORAL_MARKER]`** (HOUSEKEEPING.md:52, Apartment.groovy:44): the room shows its apartment's
   era marker first among its tags (Decision 7: a label that never showed is made to show). Guide:311-313 said
   "nothing on screen announces it"; now the room does. Goldens and the tag counts moved (5 → 6).
4. **The Keystone at 0 Hz** already non-resonant (I06); a forged Keystone does not count in the tally, whatever
   Guide:247 says the old counter did.
5. **"Never stop"** (Guide:277-278, Building.groovy:255-261): the substrate is **ten Layers deep** — the pressure
   readout saturates at −10 (Guide:505-506) — because an endless list has no home under the lazy-loading law
   (children are generated once). The Guide's *feel* is kept: ten Layers under a three-floor building.
6. **The Guide's `s` costs 1 like everything** (Guide:134): a GLOBAL turn (no step); so the lottery never rolls
   on a scan (Guide:189-190). Verified in `Descent.test`.
7. **The breach is not a MoveTable move**: it consumes a fragment, so it is a journey transaction like a capture,
   offered as a move (`role: 'move'`, key `j`) when the floor says so — in both modes (HK-018).
8. **The old `[DATA_SUMMARY]` / `[SENSORY_TELEMETRY]` are one panel**: each row carries its sensory line under it;
   an empty inscription shows no bare label (seen on the desktop shot). The panel lasts one step and is not saved.
9. **The echo hunt is saved** (Guide:369-370 said the old save did not): `{signal}` / `{found}` on the reach.
10. **`(No echo) e` after the capture**: nothing is offered — Guide:194 "one echo per reach, ever" — but a reboot
    rebuilds the world, so the reach forgets, as the old game's `initializeWorld` did.
11. **Hidden Frequency and Spectral Echo never resonate**: Guide:245-247 names the culture match and the merge as
    the only badges; a prize that happens to be a multiple of 11 is not counted (Player.groovy counted nothing on
    `capture` either).
12. **The `☠` map marks** (Guide:331-332) are I08: only the Shard's path icon is `☠` here, as the sparkline did.
