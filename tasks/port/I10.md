## Choices the brief did not dictate

- **The playthrough starts at the real title** (NEW WORLD draws a seed from the browser's entropy) and then swaps the
  drawn seed for the fixed world (`7F3A-91C2-0B4D-E6A8`) by writing the save exactly as the game stores a drawn,
  unentered world, so ENTER WORLD lands on Bright Boulevard and every later assertion is a literal. A random world
  cannot promise a room with four relics on floor 0.
- **"Reload continues" means a reload after CONTINUE**, not at the title: once a place is saved, a reload never stops
  at the title screen (`GameEngine.#restore`) — the first run of the spec failed on my assumption, the game was right.
- **The ritual is PRIME + a real merge** (the eighth), not SPAWN KEYSTONE: the Keystone comes out of the buffer screen
  as a player would earn it; the breach and the descent are then tapped for real, fifteen floors up.
- **Coherence is asserted as literals at six points** (94 in the room, 92 after two takes, 100 after the merge —
  capped —, 97 after SCAN + EXIT + MAP, 83 on the Peak, 79 on Layer −1, 77 after the recap's double cost); the buffer
  and tile counts allow for the lottery.
- **The docs' citations are `web/`-relative `src/…:a-b`** in HTML comments, as the terminal guide's are
  `File.groovy:a-b`, and a script (`cite.py`, scratchpad) read every range back from the file: six were wrong on the
  first pass (a `cat -n` over several files had shifted them) and were fixed before commit.
- **The pointer in the terminal guide is appended to line 15**, not inserted, so lines 1–103 keep their numbering
  (`GlobalCommandsContractTest.groovy:9` cites `:102-103`); `wc -l` is still 519.
- **The nav gains `[PLAY]` and `[WEB_GUIDE]`** before `[GUIDE]` (the nav is hand-written in `_layouts/terminal.html`,
  not data-driven); `.nav-play` gets a cyan style beside the other three coloured entries.
- **`docs/web/` is safe under Jekyll**: no `_` prefix, not in `_config.yml`'s `exclude`, the same `layout: terminal`
  front matter and `relative_url` links as `docs/terminal/guide/`. Jekyll is not installed here (`which bundle
  jekyll ruby gem` → nothing), so the build was verified by reading: front matter fields match the terminal cheat
  sheet's four, every `relative_url` target resolves to a page under `docs/` (one script), `docs/play/` untouched.
- **The README's Groovy version is 5**, as `installation_guide.md:14` says (the old README badge said 4.x).
