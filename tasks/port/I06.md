## Guide-vs-code disagreements (Decision 6: the Guide wins) and what the Guide does not say

1. **The buffer's capacity.** Guide:238 and 409: "there is no cap", the HUD's `n/16` lies (HUDHeaderComponent.groovy:99).
   Decision 7 says a label that lied is made true, and the brief asked for a capacity: **sixteen is the cap**. A full
   buffer lists the room's takes sealed (shown, not tappable) with a note; a merge or a drop makes room.
2. **The hybrid's name order.** Guide:241-243: "Rusted Chain" and "Paper Lantern" become `Rusted-Paper Hybrid` — the
   order given. Player.groovy:87-89 took the *higher* index first (`m 1 4` gave "4th-1st"). Done the Guide's way: the
   first pick is first.
3. **A dropped relic keeps everything.** Guide:121, 452 warn that only the name survives (Room.groovy:208); Decision 7
   fixes it: a fragment carries its provenance and comes back as it was — a hybrid too, a Keystone (I07) too.
4. **The tally counts once.** Room.groovy:227 counted a drop-and-retake again; here only a *fresh* capture counts.
5. **0 Hz is not resonant.** Player.groovy:106-107 via SpectralFrequency.groovy:16; `Frequency.resonant()` excludes 0.
6. **"stabilized" / "Stable:"** (SessionRecap.groovy:46, TelemetryComponent.groovy:142) count resonant traces; the
   lines say so now.
7. **The buffer screen's destroy** (`d 3`, Guide:126, QuantumBufferController.groovy:38) is not carried: with a capacity
   and a drop that keeps the frequency, the room drop covers every use; nothing is ever destroyed.
8. **Auto-take** (Guide:74: one object, empty buffer, `t` takes it) is not carried: a tile is one tap already.
9. **The `t` menu's cost.** In the old game a drop cost the `t` prompt (1 + a step); here a drop is an answer of the
   buffer screen and costs nothing — opening the buffer cost the one (Guide:134).
10. **The session's `[NEW_SYNTHESIS]` labels** (InventoryOverlayComponent.groovy:40-44, cleared on leaving the screen,
    QuantumBufferController.groovy:28) are not carried: the status line names the hybrid just made.
11. **Not in this iteration:** the Hidden Frequency lottery (Guide:187-190, Room.groovy:70-72) and the Null Reach echo —
    not in the I06 goal; both are one more source of fragments (a `kind` for the reader) and a turn-seeded roll. The
    Keystone (Guide:251-252) is I07's: `Frequency.resonant()` already refuses its 0 Hz, and the reader's table takes
    its row.

## Choices the brief did not dictate

- **Provenance instead of stored hertz.** A relic fragment's save form is `{ from, key }`: the room it came from says
  what it is worth. So a save can hold nothing a room could not have produced, a hybrid is exactly as producible as
  its parts, and "keeps its frequency" is structural. The honest limit: sixteen copies of one relic from one room pass
  (each is producible on its own); the check is per fragment, not per world.
- **A room's memento is `{ taken, dropped }`**, keyed by the room, not the apartment (the I05 note guessed the
  apartment): what lies *here* is the room's fact; the deal stays the apartment's.
- **The buffer is a pending prompt that takes several answers** (`Reply.done`), the third screen after the reboot and
  the recap, and a full screen rather than a `<dialog>`: the shell already owns focus, scroll and the live region
  for a scene change, and BACK TO REALITY sits in a sticky dock in thumb reach.
- **Merge is two taps** (select, then merge on another row): options are data, and n(n−1)/2 pair buttons is not a
  phone screen. Picking the selected row again unselects.
- **Role names:** `take` (a room's object), `pick`, `drop` — the presenters sort by role and place row actions by
  `ordinal`, never by id.
- **Words:** `Captured <name>. Frequency: <n> Hz.` (+ `Harmonic resonance: +10%.` when it counted), `Synthesis
  complete: <name> (<n> Hz). Coherence +15.` (+ `Resonance detected.`), `Dropped <name> here.`, `BUFFER FULL — merge
  or drop a fragment to take more.`, the old `(No spectral traces detected in local buffer)`, `SYNC_STATUS: NOMINAL`.
- **`i` is claimed** like the other command keys: a street's twentieth building is keyed `X` now.
- **`terminal/` untouched**; `web/CLAUDE.md` gained the items paragraph and the save shape.
