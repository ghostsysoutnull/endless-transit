## Choices the brief did not dictate

- **The dock on a phone is one row: LEAVE and MORE.** I08's four-plus-MORE was three rows of 56 px — a sticky
  block of 200 px, a third of a 640 px screen and the biggest thief of the first screen. SCAN, MAP, BUFFER,
  TRACE, HELP, TITLE SCREEN and END SESSION wait behind MORE, rising above the LEAVE row and sharing rows by
  their width (two rows); the fold closes on the next step. A desktop shows all eight in the dock's row and no
  MORE (`@media (min-width: 900px)`), so every button is always in the markup and a key always works.
- **The moves sit under the title on both layouts**, not only on the phone: one DOM, one rule, and on the
  desktop the strip reads as the place's controls above its words.
- **A panel comes after the list on a phone**, and the spotlight rule brings it into view: this keeps the
  first screen the place's own (the brief's rule 1) while rule 2 holds. On a street the MAP panel repeats the
  pane's map below the list (I08's pane map is full width on a phone) — left as is.
- **The HUD's fold keeps two crumbs**, the steps, the buffer and the place's position (`Z-AXIS 01/16`), and
  says `… ›` before the first kept crumb; the locus, hash, seed and depth open on the readout button (`⋯` / `×`,
  named "The whole readout", `aria-expanded`) and stay open across steps — a player who opened it chose to.
- **HELP costs one** (Guide:96 lists it among the commands; Guide:133 "every prompt costs") and is keyed `h`; the
  way back is free like the buffer's. The manual names the buttons as the screen shows them and repeats no key
  (a button carries its own letter; keys are a desktop extra, Decision 1). Words: `[OPERATOR_MANUAL]`, MOVING,
  THE DOCK, HOW NOT TO DIE, `▲ BACK TO THE WORLD`.
- **The debug strip's fold stays open** across steps (a tester taps several tools in a row); the toggle and
  every tool carry `tabindex="-1"`.
- **`fold.spec`'s rule:** the first `button[data-option]` outside the dock and the debug strip, in DOM order,
  wholly inside the viewport and above the sticky dock's top, with the page at its top; a screen with none
  (the link failure) shows its one button. Both profiles; the phone also at 360 × 640.
