# I03 — Buildings

**Plan (as run):** the model's new questions (`listing`, `arrival`, `exit`/`leave`, `moves`/`move`, `remember`/`recall`,
`startOfJourney`) + doors per size → building, floors, the two floor states → corridor, doors, apartments, rooms → the
rules (street start, moves, leave, save v3) → the HUD (moves strip, floor readings, door rows) → browser tests in both
profiles → this note. Six commits, one per module with its tests, RED then GREEN. A rewrite guided by the Groovy source
and the Player's Guide (the Guide wins, Decision 6); none of the study §2.2 designs is copied: no closure menus, no list
interception, no static state, no model calling UI, no sleeps (the Groovy `Building.enter` / `breach` pauses are gone —
nothing in the UI needs a delay either), no player state read inside generation.

## Where the traveller may stand

Building, floor (at the elevator or in the corridor), room. **Corridor** and **apartment** are real kinds in the tree
(crumbs, addresses `…floor.0.door.room`, registry entries) but nobody stands in them: `Corridor.arrival()` is its floor
and `Apartment.arrival()` its first room (the auto-entry rule, Guide:73, `NavigationOrchestrator.groovy:184-194`). So the
old game's second, plainer corridor screen after leaving an apartment (Guide:114, HK-021) does not exist: leaving the first
room lands on the floor in its corridor mode, doors listed, `BACK TO ELEVATOR` and `LEAVE FLOOR` at hand.

## Guide-vs-code disagreements (Decision 6: the Guide wins) and what is left of I02's list

1. **Where the game starts** (I02 #2, the one place the Guide did not win yet): done the Guide's way — a new world lands
   on a street (Guide:41). `Journey.enter` asks `universe.startOfJourney()`.
2. **The keys in the lobby.** Guide:111 "the key is the floor number, so `0` is the ground floor". In the web game keys are a
   desktop extra handed out in list order (Decision 1; I02's rule), so the Peak is `1` and the lobby the 16th key. The
   floor's *number* is what the row shows (its ordinal), which is what the Guide's sentence is about; the key convention
   of the terminal is not carried. Recorded as a choice, not a rule broken.
3. **Door words override.** `Door.groovy:170` rolls its own inscription and `CorridorFactory.groovy:170` rolls again on the
   same branch and overwrites it — the same 20 % roll, so the contextual one always wins. Guide:222-224 describes the
   contextual result. Done the Guide's way, one roll.
4. **"A building needs nine floors to have all three"** (Guide:358) is about the three executive floors, not three tiers:
   at nine floors 1–4 are basement, 5–7 executive, none living. Pinned as read (`Floors.test.ts`).
5. **`(VISITED)` on a door** (Guide:412, `Door.visited` never set — HK-021): not carried; visited marks are I05.
6. The rest of I02's list (planet cultures, second culture/era always different, rebel decided at creation) stands.

## Choices the brief did not dictate

- **Corridor and apartment stay in the tree but are never stood in** (`arrival()`), instead of a floor whose "children"
  are apartments: the crumbs and the sparkline keep `▤ ▅ 🚪 □`, addresses stay structural, and the state pattern is
  still the floor's (`FloorState`), as the brief asked.
- **`exit()` is pure, `leave()` acts**: the engine decides whether to offer LEAVE by asking `exit()`; only running it calls
  `leave()`, which is where the floor returns itself to the elevator. No option list ever changes state.
- **The default `exit()` is the parent's `arrival()`** — an apartment's way out is the floor, not the corridor, without
  any kind naming another.
- **A `Location.move(id)` a kind offers but does not make throws** — a contract check instead of a silent no-op.
- **`remember()` / `recall()` are generic**: the save holds `states: { address: memento }` for every place on the trail
  that remembers something; today only a floor in corridor mode. A place's memento is its own text form. Only the trail
  is saved: at most one floor can be in corridor mode at a time (leaving resets it, and up/down are elevator moves), so
  nothing off the trail carries state yet.
- **The engine owns the move keys** (`MOVE_KEYS` u d c b f, Guide:112-115), the model owns the moves. The children's
  letters lose those five: a 15-street city now reads `123456789aghijk` (pin moved, `GameEngine.test.ts`).
- **Ordinal and readings ride on the option** (`GameOption.ordinal`, `.readings`): the presenter never computes a
  number from a row's position — a floor goes by its own number.
- **Floor zones, room kinds and colours became content** (`names/floors/**`, `names/rooms/<Trait>` with the guaranteed
  door words as `<style> <WORD>`, `themes/colours`) — words are content, as I02 did with names. `names/rooms` is keyed by
  trait the way `walls/` is keyed by culture; the bundle test knows.
- **Not done, on the brief's word:** furniture, objects, the anomaly glitch and the abyssal words (I04 owns room text and
  objects), scans and door traces (I07: the trace table of Guide:207-215 is scan-only, so `RoomCategory` carries no
  trace yet), visited marks and the `[>X<]` current-floor mark of the elevator column (I05, with the visited path), the
  Null Reach echo (I06). No room text depends on the step count at these levels (the Hidden Frequency roll is a capture,
  I06); nothing was deferred for lack of a step count.
- **Timing:** the Groovy landmark and breach pauses are simply not there; the landmark banner is a red tag on the
  building's panel (`UNIQUE_LOCUS_DETECTION MAJOR_LANDMARK_DISCOVERED`), shown every visit until visited state exists.
- **Words:** the building panel says "Analyzing vertical lattice structure..." under the heading BUILDING STRATA
  DIAGNOSTICS; the corridor's heading LOCAL ACCESS LIST; the elevator's second line "Local signal is STABLE. Corridor
  access authorized." — all the source's. A move's status line is its label when the place is the same ("Enter
  Corridor.", "Back to Elevator."), "Entered X." when it is not.
- **`terminal/` untouched**; `web/CLAUDE.md` gained the new questions, the state rule, the option shape and the save
  shape (laws that changed).

**The tester can try:** open the page on a phone held upright and on a desktop. Tap **NEW WORLD**, then **ENTER
WORLD**: you stand on a **street** (the universe is still up there — **▲ LEAVE STREET** seven times). Tap a building: its
floors are listed top floor first, each with its zone, integrity and resonance. Tap **Lobby** (the last row, `00`): you
are at the elevator — **GO UP**, **GO DOWN** and **ENTER CORRIDOR** are the buttons under the panel, **▲ LEAVE FLOOR**
at the bottom. Ride up two floors and enter the corridor: the doors are listed with their material, state and, on about
one in five, an inscription — `[DATA_VAULT]` always opens on a Laboratory or a Bio-Server, `!! DANGER !!` on a Security
Station or an Armory. Tap a door: you are in its first room, with its type, oxygen, temperature and signal and two lines
about the place. **GO FORWARD** / **GO BACK** walk the apartment's rooms; **▲ EXIT APARTMENT** (first room only) puts you
back in the corridor. **▲ LEAVE FLOOR** from the corridor takes you to the building, and that floor opens at the
elevator next time. Close the tab anywhere — in a room, in a corridor — and come back: you are where you were. On a
desktop: `U` / `D` ride the elevator, `C` enters the corridor, `B` goes back, `F` forward, digits pick a floor or a door,
`L` leaves.
