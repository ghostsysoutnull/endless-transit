## Choices the brief did not dictate

- **A relic's key is its form and parts**, not a position in the deck: `with|tatami mat|floppy disk`. A reordered list
  keeps every identity; a renamed item is a new relic (as it would be a new frequency in I06).
- **The apartment owns the dealing** (`relicsIn`) and its rooms ask — "objects live in apartments" made literal; a room's
  object list is derived, and I06's taking will be the apartment's memento (`remember`/`recall`), not the room's.
- **The room count is decided at creation** (`Progeny.count`) so `relicsIn` can be asked before the rooms exist; the same
  `children` branch as before, so no I03 pin moved.
- **The deal is one pass** (`Deal.take`): dealing nineteen of a thousand card by card was fine for a room's adjective,
  not for three thousand apartments in a test.
- **The glitch is seeded on the room** (`branch('static')`) — a glitched line reads the same every time the room is
  drawn; the Groovy rolled the clock and it flickered. Recorded, not a disagreement: nothing pins a flicker.
- **The fallback key is the index's first**, not the Groovy's literal `monolith`: one rule for three categories, never
  silent. On the bundled content no warning ever fires (`RoomContents.test.ts:151`); the browser tests fail on a console
  warning, so a `[THEME_WARN]` in the game is a red test.
- **Furniture in the panel, objects beside it**: FURNITURE and OBJECTS_DETECTED are rows under the interpretation
  (the Groovy lines); the objects themselves are the mock's tiles in the aside, so on a desktop the right column is
  filled on a room screen and a phone reads them under the moves. A tile is not a button (no dead buttons): I06 makes it one.
- **The telemetry block on every screen indoors** (building too, under its floor list) — the Groovy pane's rule is depth,
  restated as `indoors()`; outdoors the pane is the map, I08. `[SYSTEM_TELEMETRY]`'s words are the presenter's.
- **A door's appearance rides as a reading** (`Fact` key `narrative`, label APPEARANCE read out), in sans under the name.
  A twenty-door corridor grows tall on a phone; the name reads first, the prose is dim. For the tester to judge.
- **Words:** `IN THIS ROOM` (the mock's), `No objects detected.` for an empty room, the RESONANCE tag's `stable` key is the
  one new colour (green, `--gn`, contrast test green).
- **`terminal/` untouched**; `web/CLAUDE.md` gained the goldens rule, the `WarningSink`, and the apartments-hold-objects law.
