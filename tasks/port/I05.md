## Guide-vs-code disagreements (Decision 6: the Guide wins) and what the Guide does not say

1. **The drain follows the street header** (Guide:313), while `TurnProcessor.groovy:51-52` reads `state.currentLocation.getVibe()` —
   in Groovy an apartment's drifted era could change the cost. Done the Guide's way: a room's `vibe()` is its street's,
   so the street's era drives the drain everywhere below it.
2. **"You begin on a street, already a point or two down: the drain runs before the first screen is drawn"** (Guide:67)
   is the terminal loop's artefact (`Game.groovy:93-95`: the drain opens every iteration, the input closes it). In the web
   the drain is the tap's: entering a new world shows **100** (Guide:43 is the rule), and the first tap shows 99 —
   the same "99/98/96" pattern from the second screen on (`CoherenceDrainTest.groovy:29,40,50,60`).
3. **What zero does at the edge:** in Groovy the last command runs and the next loop's drain kills (`Game.groovy:95`,
   `TurnProcessor.groovy:55`); here the tap that takes the last point never runs its command (the brief's order). The
   displayed sequence is the same (a screen at 1, a tap, the failure); the step count differs by one at death.
4. **Reboot coherence:** `TurnProcessor.groovy:99` adds 100 to a value floored at 0 — always 100; the Guide says 100
   (Guide:145). Same either way; `Player.reboot()` sets it outright.
5. **"You keep your buffer"** (Guide:145): there is no buffer yet (I06); the reboot keeps what exists — steps and the
   visited path — and `Player.reboot()` is where the buffer will be kept too.
6. **Visited marks on the map** ("dim symbols are unvisited", Guide:340) are I08's; the `[CLEARED]` / `[n/total]` floor
   progress of `Building.groovy:224-228` is not carried (it needs every floor's sub-tree generated to count) — recorded,
   not deferred to a row.
7. **The recap's `BUFFER_DENSITY` and `RESONANT_TRACES`** lines (`SessionRecap.groovy:44-46`) wait for I06, as the
   `> Stable:` line did in I04; the "stabilized" mislabel (HK-023) is not copied.
8. **A typo costs 1** (Guide:134): buttons cannot mistype; a stale tap costs nothing.
9. Below-bedrock relabel (INTEGRITY) and the void ending are I07: the multiplier (`drainFactor`) and the endings table
   are the hooks.

## Choices the brief did not dictate

- **The Journey owns the traveller** (`Journey.player()`): "where the traveller stands" and the traveller's record of it
  are one save and one restore; every landing is a footprint in one place (`#land`). The engine only does the turn's
  accounting (drain, count). A separate `Expedition` class would have split `saved()` / `restore()` in two.
- **The per-place slot is `states`, now keyed over the visited path** rather than the trail: I03's "a building's elevator
  floor is forgotten off the trail" is gone — it survives (the Groovy saved `lastVisitedFloor` for every visited building
  too). I06's taken relics are the apartment's memento here; a breach flag (HK-023) is the building's. No second
  `mutations` key: one owner per fact.
- **Strict restore grew three rules** (visited addresses resolve, parents before children, the trail is held) and one
  moved (a state's owner must be visited, not on the trail). The Journey tests show each refused; the sessions test shows
  `saved(restore(s))` after every tap of four fixtures and 360 random taps.
- **The title screen is not a saved state** (as I02: a reload lands in the world), nor is the recap; the reboot is
  (coherence 0 ⇔ the link is down): a reload finds the failure screen again.
- **`to-title` costs one** (a global command, like `i`), `Continue` costs nothing (returning from a sub-screen); `q`
  costs one to open the recap, its answers cost nothing (the old `y/N` reads).
- **The debug INTEGRITY is a ladder** (100 / 70 / 69 / 40 / 39 / 30 / 29 / 1): options are data, there is no number
  input; the edges of every band, full, and one from failure. It is free (a tool, not a prompt — in Groovy setting 40
  showed 39). `?debug` on the page's address is the flag (`main.ts`); nowhere else.
- **Words:** `COHERENCE` with the band's name after the value (STABLE / DEGRADED / CRITICAL, the meter's `aria-valuetext`
  "87 percent, stable"), `PULSE_TRAVERSAL` (the Guide's name; the mock says MOVES), `END SESSION` / `RESUME` / `REBUILD`,
  the failure screen's `NEURAL LINK LOST` stage line, `Substrate rebuilt. Coherence 100.` as the reborn status.
- **Look:** the mock's instruments row for the meter (a thin bar filled with the band colour, animated by CSS width,
  off under reduced motion); the failure screen is the title's stage in the mock's dead frame; the recap is the old
  terminal block in the place's frame.
- **`terminal/` untouched**; `web/CLAUDE.md` gained the turn, the save shape, the debug rule and the fixture format.
