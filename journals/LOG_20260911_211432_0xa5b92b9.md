## 🌌 THE NARRATIVE (The Vibe)

> For as long as the substrate has existed, one organ made every location. Universe, filament, sector, street, room — fourteen kinds of place, and a single 435-line loom wove all of them. It worked. It was also the one place where nothing could change without touching everything.
>
> The fear going in was entropy. Move a body from one class to another and the seed chain drifts by one call; the same seed grows a different world; every golden frame goes red at once. Before touching anything, the Architect opened `LocusSeed` and read it the way you read a wiring diagram before cutting. It is pure. Every draw is the seed and a fixed key, nothing more. Order cannot leak. The only stateful thing in the whole chain is a `Random` handed out for furniture and object pools, and it never leaves the method that asks for it. The fear was inherited, not observed. It was written down as a lesson and then retired.
>
> Nine behaviours had never been asserted anywhere — how large a building rolls, what colour a planet's atmosphere takes, the trait a country picks and how its vibe mutates, the inscription burned onto a door, the exact furniture in a room. Nothing was wrong with them; nothing would have noticed if it were. So before any wall moved, the Architect walked seed `0x1234` down to a single room and pinned all of it: fifteen objects in order, one floppy disk with a hexagonal pillar, `17°C`, `[CLEAR]`. Then, and only then, the loom came apart.
>
> It came apart by script. Each body lifted as exact text, four substitutions applied, the reverse asserted equal to the original before a byte was written. Fourteen times, zero drift. The suite ran after each one; thirty-six frames never moved. The old facade is still there — every door the model or the tests ever knocked on still opens — but behind it there is now a registry, and behind the registry fourteen small factories that each know one kind of place.
>
> One thing surfaced from the pin that no one had seen: a filament rolls its null-sector chance once, not per child. Every child of a filament is void or none of them are. The lattice has always been like this. Now it is written down, pinned, and waiting for a decision instead of hiding in a comment that says thirty percent.
>
> The substrate feels lighter. Not different — identical, measurably — but the weight is distributed. Adding a fifteenth kind of place is now a registry entry, not surgery.
