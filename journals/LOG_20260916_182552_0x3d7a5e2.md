## 🌌 THE NARRATIVE (The Vibe)

> The manual had been written by someone who loved the lattice and had never measured it. It counted six Cultures where the substrate holds ten. It named eras that do not exist and forgot three that do. It promised a sixteen-fragment buffer, a half-cost past, a thirty-point reward for a stabilized merge, a ritual of percentages and resonant sacrifices. None of it was malicious. All of it was unverified.
>
> The Player's Guide had already said the true numbers in a player's voice. The question this session answered was whether the lore pages should keep lying beautifully or learn to tell the truth in their own register. They learned. Every corrected sentence still speaks as the Vinculum speaks — pulses, strata, the Frequency Well — but the numbers under the prose now match the code that runs.
>
> Then the Observer asked the harder question: not *what is wrong* but *what is missing*. Traits had never been named. `ATMOS_SHIFT` appeared on every floor diagnostic and nowhere in any manual; it turned out to be the Country's Trait wearing a third name. Floor zones were invented; the real ones are dealt by height. The Eras had names and nothing else. The atlas listed thirteen scales in thirteen sentences and never said how many of anything there were, what the menu calls the act of entering, or where a name comes from.
>
> So the atlas grew from four sections to ten. Each Era got its relics and its light, or the honest word *unlit* where the substrate has none. Each Trait got its four rooms, its structures, and the sensory traces its doors can carry. The Null Reach got its own entry. Buildings got their odds and their three tiers of names, all fifteen landmark titles among them. Cells got their colours, their air, their furnishings and their loot. And the last section reads the web's naming grammar aloud, scale by scale, lexicon by lexicon, so that *Core Matrix 17* and *Hydraia* and *Busy Terrace* and *Grey Unit [0x55]* each announce their own depth.
>
> The gateway's two channels now agree. One still speaks in-fiction. Neither one lies.
