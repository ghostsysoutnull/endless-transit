# OPERATIONAL_PROTOCOLS: System Mechanics

To survive the Transit, an Observer must master the fundamental laws of the Neural Web.

## [COHERENCE & INTEGRITY]
Your **Coherence** represents the stability of your connection to the lattice.
*   **The Drain:** Every movement (changing locations) costs a base amount of Coherence. 
*   **Temporal Variance:** Ancient/Analog timelines drain Coherence slowly (0.5x), while Entropic/Singularity timelines drain it rapidly (2.0x).
*   **Restoration:** Coherence is primarily restored by **Synthesizing Fragments** in your buffer.
*   **Integrity:** In the Abyssal Substrate, your connection is under high pressure. This is displayed as **Integrity**—if it fails, the link is severed.

## [GEMATRIA: THE FREQUENCY ENGINE]
Objects found within the lattice aren't just "items"—they are spectral signatures. 
*   **Scanning:** Every object has a frequency measured in **Hertz (Hz)**.
*   **Harmonic Resonance:** If an object is scanned in a room matching the planet's primary culture, its frequency is amplified by +10%.
*   **Master Numbers:** If an item's frequency hits a "Master Number" (11, 22, 33...) during extraction, it is considered **Resonant** and provides a bonus to your waveform stability.

## [THE HARMONIC INVERSION RITUAL]
To access the Abyssal Substrate, you must perform a ritual at the "Peak" of a building:
1.  **Strata Sampling:** Visit a variety of floors within a building to collect atmospheric data.
2.  **Infusion:** Use resonant fragments from your buffer to "infuse" the building's anchor point.
3.  **Synthesis:** Combine the data and infusions into a **Building Keystone**.
4.  **The Breach:** With the Keystone in hand, you can initiate a Bedrock Breach, opening an aperture to the negative strata below Floor 0.
