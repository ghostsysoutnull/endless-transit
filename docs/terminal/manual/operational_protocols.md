# OPERATIONAL_PROTOCOLS: System Mechanics

To survive the Transit, an Observer must master the fundamental laws of the Neural Web.

## [COHERENCE & INTEGRITY]
Your **Coherence** represents the stability of your connection to the lattice.
*   **The Drain:** Every pulse of the link — every directive you issue, whether it moves you or not — costs **1 Coherence**. A scan costs the same as a step.
*   **Temporal Variance:** In an **Entropic** Era the cost doubles to 2. No Era drains more slowly than the base rate.
*   **Restoration:** Coherence is restored only by **Synthesizing Fragments** in your buffer: **+15 per synthesis**, capped at 100.
*   **Integrity:** In the Abyssal Substrate, your connection is under high pressure. Coherence is displayed as **Integrity** and every pulse costs double (4 in an Entropic Era). If it reaches zero, the link reboots: the web is regenerated from the same seed and you wake on the starting street with your buffer intact and the world's memory of you erased.

## [GEMATRIA: THE FREQUENCY ENGINE]
Objects found within the lattice aren't just "items"—they are spectral signatures. 
*   **Scanning:** Every object has a frequency measured in **Hertz (Hz)**, derived from the consonants of its name and the depth at which it was found.
*   **Harmonic Resonance:** If an object is captured in a room matching the planet's primary culture, its frequency is amplified by +10%.
*   **Master Numbers:** If the raw consonant sum of a name is exactly 11, 22 or 33, the engine announces `RESONANCE DETECTED` and doubles it before scaling.
*   **Stabilized Waveforms:** Any frequency divisible by 11 is **Stabilized**. Stabilized captures and syntheses are counted in your `RESONANT_TRACES` tally. They confer no other bonus.

## [THE HARMONIC INVERSION RITUAL]
To access the Abyssal Substrate, you must prime a building and breach it from its Peak:
1.  **Strata Sampling:** Capture at least one fragment on **every floor** of the building. A floor with no capture is unsampled.
2.  **Infusion:** Perform **seven syntheses while inside the building**. Syntheses performed elsewhere do not infuse it.
3.  **Synthesis:** Once sampled and infused, the **next synthesis inside the building** yields the **Building Keystone** — the eighth merge, counting from a cold building.
4.  **The Breach:** Ride to the Peak with the Keystone in your buffer. The `j` directive initiates the Bedrock Breach, consuming the Keystone and opening an aperture below Floor 0.
