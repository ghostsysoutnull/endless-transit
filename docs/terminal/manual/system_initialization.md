# SYSTEM_INITIALIZATION: The Neural Link

## [OVERVIEW]
You are not a traveler. You are an **Observer**. 

Your physical form remains anchored in the Unimatrix Root, while your consciousness is projected across the **Neural Web**—an infinite, procedural lattice of interconnected realities. This is **Endless Transit**.

## [THE TRANSIT]
Long ago, the Lattice was a stable construct of data and light. But the "Entropy Shift" has begun. The world is decaying into static, and the once-stable cities are sinking into the **Abyssal Substrate**. 

"The Transit" refers to the forced migration of consciousness across this crumbling web. To stay ahead of the decay, you must move. To move, you must remain coherent.

## [YOUR OBJECTIVE]
As an Observer, your primary protocols are:
1.  **Harvest Spectral Fragments:** Scanned objects contain unique frequencies (Hz). Collect them to stabilize your waveform.
2.  **Maintain Coherence:** Every step through the lattice drains your neural link. If your coherence hits 0%, the link is severed and your trace is lost to the void.
3.  **Synthesis:** Use the **Quantum Trace Buffer** to merge fragments into powerful **Keystones**. These keys allow you to anchor yourself in the deeper strata of the web.
4.  **Explore the Bedrock:** Only by breaching the "Bedrock" of a building can you witness the raw system-code of the universe—the Abyssal Substrate.
