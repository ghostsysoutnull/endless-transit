# TECHNICAL_SUBSTRATE: The Inversion Ritual

The **Harmonic Inversion Ritual** is the only method for accessing the **Abyssal Substrate** beneath a stable building. It is a four-stage process of atmospheric data harvesting and frequency synthesis.

## [STAGE 1: STRATA_SAMPLING]
The building's anchor point must be "mapped" through physical traversal.
*   **The Requirement:** The Observer must visit at least **70% of the floors** in a building.
*   **Data Harvest:** Each floor visit records a "Structural Trace." Once enough traces are collected, the ritual interface unlocks at the building's Peak.

## [STAGE 2: FRAGMENT_INFUSION]
The building's "Frequency Well" must be primed with external data.
*   **The Requirement:** The Observer must sacrifice **7 resonant fragments** from their buffer.
*   **Resonance:** Items matching the building's local culture provide a "Purity Bonus" to the final synthesis.

## [STAGE 3: KEYSTONE_SYNTHESIS]
Once the building is sampled and infused, the data is compressed into a **Building Keystone**.
*   **The Object:** The Keystone is a high-density spectral fragment unique to that specific building.
*   **Persistence:** The Keystone is stored in the **Quantum Trace Buffer** and can be used to re-enter the building's substrate even after a neural link restoration.

## [STAGE 4: THE_BEDROCK_BREACH]
With the Keystone active, the "Breach" directive becomes available.
*   **Execution:** Initiating the breach inverts the building's vertical polarity.
*   **Transition:** The Observer descends through Floor 0 into the **Abyssal Layers**. 
*   **System Shift:** Standard terminology (Room, Floor) is replaced by substrate terminology (Shard, Layer).
