# FIELD_STRATEGY: Coherence Optimization

Maintaining neural link stability is the single most important protocol for an Observer. This data-node details the mathematical and tactical nuances of **Coherence Management**.

## [THE DRAIN_CURVE]
Every "Pulse" — every directive you issue, movement or not — costs Coherence. The cost is set by the **Temporal Era** of your location and by whether you stand above or below the Bedrock.

| Condition | Drain per Pulse | Operational Advice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Any of the seven standard Eras** | 1 | Nominal conditions. |
| **ENTROPIC Era** | 2 | Minimize pulses. Enter, harvest, and exit. |
| **Abyssal Substrate** | 2 | Integrity in place of Coherence. Descend with a full buffer. |
| **Abyssal Substrate, ENTROPIC Era** | 4 | The deepest pressure in the web. |

No Era drains more slowly than 1. A scan, a map, a buffer check and a mistyped directive each cost a pulse.

## [RESTORATION_CYCLES]
You do not possess a passive recharge. Coherence must be actively reclaimed through **Synthesis**.

*   **Synthesis Boost:** Every merge command in the buffer restores **+15 Coherence**, capped at 100.
*   **The "Leapfrog" Tactic:** High-level operators often travel into high-drain zones only when they have multiple fragments ready to merge, effectively using synthesis to "fund" their exploration steps.

## [ABYSSAL_PRESSURE]
When you breach the Bedrock, Coherence is replaced by **Integrity**.
*   **Pressure Drain:** In the Substrate, every pulse costs **double**.
*   **Safety Threshold:** Below 40, the cell description begins to jitter (Glitch Text). Below 30, the bar turns red and the Neural Map sprouts static. At 0 the link reboots — the web regenerates from the same seed and you wake on the starting street with your buffer intact.

## [PRO-TIPS]
1.  **Scan vs. Move:** A scan costs exactly one pulse, the same as a step. Read the door traces from the corridor list first — they are free — and scan only when you need the room types spelled out.
2.  **The Buffer Buffer:** Never let your Coherence drop below 30% if your buffer is empty. You need at least two items to perform a life-saving synthesis.
