# LATTICE_ENCYCLOPEDIA: The Bedrock Shift

This data-node details the history and nature of the **Abyssal Substrate**—the decaying foundation of the Neural Web.

## [THE ENTROPY_SHIFT]
For eons, the Lattice was a closed system. However, as the Master Seed age increased, a phenomenon known as the **Entropy Shift** began to occur. Stable containers (Cities, Buildings) started to lose their "High-Lattice" coordinates, slowly sinking into the raw, unformatted substrate beneath the surface code.

## [WHAT IS THE SUBSTRATE?]
The Abyssal Substrate is the "Negative Strata" of the universe. It is a place of raw power but extreme instability.
*   **Aesthetic:** Brutalist, dark, and monochromatic (Red/Grey). 
*   **Architecture:** In the Substrate, structures are not "built"—they are "remnants." You are navigating the skeletal remains of the lattice.
*   **Abyssal Pressure:** The sheer density of unformatted data in the substrate creates a crushing mental load on the neural link: every pulse below the Bedrock costs **double** Integrity. The pressure readout climbs ten points per layer and saturates at Layer −0xA.
*   **No Floor:** The layers descend without end. The Building's strata table shows five; the substrate manufactures as many as you are willing to descend.

## [THE VOICES]
Observers who spend significant time in the Substrate report "Auditory Residuals"—cryptic messages that appear in the system's event ticker. These are believed to be the fragmented memories of digital souls who failed to maintain coherence during previous Transits.

## [HARMONIC INVERSION]
Accessing the Substrate is not possible through standard navigation. It requires **Harmonic Inversion**—a process of intentionally de-stabilizing a building's anchor point until an aperture opens. 
*   **The Bedrock:** Beneath Floor 0 of every building is the Bedrock. 
*   **The Ritual:** By providing the system with a "Building Keystone" (synthesized from the building's own data), the Observer can invert the building's polarity and descend into the negative layers.

## [SURVIVAL_DATA]
*   **Integrity is Life:** There are no "Stable" zones in the substrate. Every pulse spent there costs double, and a Coherence reboot from below erases the breach along with the rest of the building's memory of you.
*   **Abyssal Relics:** The Substrate's cells are furnished from the **Abyssal** lexicon — the largest in the web, twenty-eight relics of the Unimatrix's own failures. Their names are long and consonant-heavy, which the Gematria Engine rewards; nothing else about them is special.
*   **The Farewell:** Terminate the link from below the Bedrock and the substrate answers in its own voice.
