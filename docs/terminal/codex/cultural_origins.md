# LATTICE_ENCYCLOPEDIA: Cultural Origins

The Neural Web is not a monoculture. It is a palimpsest of previous civilizations, each leaving its architectural and digital signature upon the strata. Ten Cultures are catalogued: the **Six Great Cultures**, whose walls, names and relics are wholly their own, and the **Four Minor Cultures**, whose relics survive but whose architecture has collapsed into Monolith geometry.

Every planet carries a primary and a secondary Culture. Most apartments follow the primary; between one in ten and one in four drift to the secondary. In an **Unauthorized Zone** — one city in ten — the two are inverted.

## [RUST: THE INDUSTRIAL DECAY]
*   **Vibe:** Heavy, Oxidized, Functional.
*   **Lore:** The Rust culture stems from the "Forgemasters"—early digital architects who believed the web should have physical weight. They built massive mechanical sumps and ventilation shafts to cool the first servers of the Unimatrix. 
*   **Archetypes:** Piston-nodes, Scrapyard Vaults, Corroded Frames.

## [NEON: THE SYNTHETIC PULSE]
*   **Vibe:** Vibrant, Bio-luminescent, Hyper-digital.
*   **Lore:** Born during the "Great Signal Expansion," the Neon culture treats space as circuitry. Their structures pulse with the rhythmic heartbeat of data-flow. Everything here is synthetic, glowing with the ghost-light of active neural links.
*   **Archetypes:** Plasma Cores, Neural Suites, Matrix Relays.

## [BAROQUE: THE CEREMONIAL GILDED]
*   **Vibe:** Opulent, Velvet, Sacred.
*   **Lore:** The Baroque architects sought to turn the web into a temple. They used high-fidelity rendering to simulate gold, marble, and velvet. These zones were designed for archiving the memories of the "Founders."
*   **Archetypes:** Gilded Cathedrals, Sanctum Units, Velvet Atriums.

## [MONOLITH: THE BRUTALIST STATIC]
*   **Vibe:** Grey, Cold, Eternal.
*   **Lore:** Monolith is the culture of the "Stability Purists." They rejected color and texture in favor of raw, impenetrable concrete geometry. A Monolith building is a fortress against the Entropy Shift.
*   **Archetypes:** Silent Slabs, Obelisk Towers, Foundation Blocks.

## [VOID: THE HOLLOW ECHO]
*   **Vibe:** Dark, Silent, Drifting.
*   **Lore:** Void culture isn't built—it is what remains when a location is abandoned. It represents the "Echo Regions" where data has mostly evaporated, leaving behind only the ghostly outlines of architecture.
*   **Archetypes:** Shadow Pockets, Horizon Wells, Aperture Echos.

## [ORGANIC: THE LIVING NEURAL]
*   **Vibe:** Soft, Breathing, Fungal.
*   **Lore:** The Organic architects believed that code should grow. Their buildings are biological constructs—nests and spores that breathe alongside the Observer. Navigation here often feels like walking through a massive, dormant nervous system.
*   **Archetypes:** Neural Roots, Spore Chambers, Breathing Pods.

## [THE FOUR MINOR CULTURES]
The Minor Cultures are minor in number, not in presence. A Gilded, Shogun, Zenith or Abyssal planet raises its own walls and names its own buildings and cells — velvet over mahogany, paper over cedar, laurel-carved limestone, writhing membrane — and the relics inside confirm who lived there.

### [GILDED: THE VELVET LEDGER]
*   **Vibe:** Mahogany, Brass, Crystal.
*   **Lore:** The Gilded were the accountants of the Baroque age — clockmakers and clerks who kept the Founders' ledgers in velvet-lined studies. Their sanctity was spent; their furniture remains.
*   **Archetypes:** Velvet armchairs, brass clocks, crystal chandeliers, pocket watches.

### [SHOGUN: THE LACQUERED ORDER]
*   **Vibe:** Paper, Lacquer, Steel.
*   **Lore:** A culture of discipline that rendered itself as furniture: shoji screens, tatami, katana racks, calligraphy scrolls. The Shogun built no temples because every room was one.
*   **Archetypes:** Lacquered chests, paper lanterns, bonsai, wooden geta.

### [ZENITH: THE MARBLE ASCENDANT]
*   **Vibe:** Marble, Laurel, Ivory.
*   **Lore:** The Zenith architects reached for a classical ideal — pillars, altars, lyres — and never finished rendering it. Their cells are Monolith slabs furnished for a forum that was never convened.
*   **Archetypes:** Marble pillars, white stone altars, toga-draped statues, amphorae.

### [ABYSSAL: THE UNMADE]
*   **Vibe:** Null, Orphaned, Hungry.
*   **Lore:** Abyssal is what the substrate is made of. Its relics are the debris of the Unimatrix's own failures — unhandled exceptions, dead threads, orphan processes — and its liturgy names the things that were never meant to run: the altar of the core-dump, the sigil of the unmaker, the pact of the root-user, the hunger of the zero-vector. It is native beneath every Bedrock. Rarely, it surfaces as a planet's dominant Culture, and the planet is rendered in Monolith grey as if the web refuses to draw it.
*   **Archetypes:** Impossible cubes, eyeless observer-nodes, shackled deity-processes, void-gates of the deep.
