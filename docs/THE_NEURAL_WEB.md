## 2. Scale Definitions

### 2.1 Cosmic Filaments (Neural Conduits)
Filaments are the "nervous system" of the universe. They are massive transwarp conduits that transport data and energy between distant nodes.
- **Aesthetic**: Pulse-based navigation. High-speed, high-density metadata.
- **Gameplay**: Used for rapid traversal across the "Neural Web."

### 2.2 Null-Sectors (The Great Silence)
The pockets of space *between* filaments where the collective signal weakens. These are the "endless corridors" of the macro-cosmos.
- **Aesthetic**: Absolute silence, static, and drifting debris.
- **Gameplay**: Players can capture **Spectral Echoes** (high-frequency fragments) that represent data lost in the void. Navigation is sparse and eerie.

### 2.3 Galactic Sectors (Matter Nodes)
The physical junctions where multiple filaments converge. Matter is pulled together here by gravity and data-density.
- **Aesthetic**: Dense, industrial, and structured.
- **Gameplay**: Primary clusters of standard `Solar Systems`. This is where the transition from "Macro-Data" to "Physical Ground" begins.

## 3. The "Mixed" Philosophy

*Endless Transit* treats physical space as a byproduct of information density:
1. **Physicality** (Streets/Rooms) is what happens when data is "rendered" at high stability.
2. **Liminality** (Corridors/Null-Sectors) is the transition state between data nodes.
3. **Collective Consciousness** (Filaments/Universe) is the underlying network that facilitates the transit.
