What to try

  • Tap the folded corner, or swipe the card sideways, to turn it to the room's words and back.
  • Turn it many times: flip, door, peel, blinds, and at low coherence static and a torn turn. The line above the keys names each one, for this mock only.
  • Drag Coherence down and keep turning: the broken turns take over.
  • Tap a doorway to walk through it. No words on the doors: a doorway full of fog is a room you have not entered; one you have entered spills its own light and carries its number; the way out keeps its colour.
  • On arrival the room's name and first sentence show, then clear. Every name is on the back of the card.
  • Tap a relic in the picture, or take it from the back of the card: it flies to the Buffer key.
  • The fourth key steps one room back toward the entrance; in the first room it reads Leave.
