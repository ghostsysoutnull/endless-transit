  • The pole runs down the middle; each level is a live node, about 80 px across. The node pictures here are stand-ins: the game keeps its own and draws them bigger.
  • Left of a node: its kind, its name, its tags. Right of it: the vibe values that level sets, written left to right. A red label is a rebel swap; a dashed one is a drift.
  • Behind the pole, pinned while it scrolls: the era, culture and trait in force at the level in the middle of the screen, huge and faint. A word slides out and the next slides in when you scroll past a level that changes it; a rebel value burns red and jolts, a drifted one turns hollow and wavers.
  • No ribbons, no drift line, no scale labels. The empty ship spots sit next to the planet and the city nodes.
