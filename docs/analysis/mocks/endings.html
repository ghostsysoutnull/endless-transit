ENDLESS TRANSIT — THE TWELVE ENDINGS

One End screen per ending, in the order they are tried: the first reached wins. The layout is the End screen as it is in the game today, with one drawing per ending in place of the live picture of the place you stand in: each a sketch in the game's line style, to judge as an idea, not as the finished drawing. The figures show on every ending. Headings and lines are drafts to judge.
