# FIELD_STRATEGY: Coherence Optimization

Maintaining neural link stability is the single most important protocol for an Observer. This data-node details the mathematical and tactical nuances of **Coherence Management**.

## [THE DRAIN_CURVE]
Every "Pulse" (movement between nodes) costs Coherence. However, the cost is not static. It is influenced by the **Temporal Era** of your current location.

| Timeline Era | Drain Rate | Operational Advice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ANCIENT / ANALOG** | 0.5x | Ideal for deep exploration and object harvesting. |
| **STANDARD / DIGITAL** | 1.0x | Nominal conditions. |
| **VOLATILE / ENTROPIC** | 2.0x | Minimize steps. Enter, scan, and exit immediately. |
| **SINGULARITY** | 2.0x | Extremely high risk. Reserved for high-level synthesis. |

## [RESTORATION_CYCLES]
You do not possess a passive recharge. Coherence must be actively reclaimed through **Synthesis**.

*   **Synthesis Boost:** Merging two fragments in the buffer restores **+15% Coherence**.
*   **The "Leapfrog" Tactic:** High-level operators often travel into high-drain zones only when they have multiple fragments ready to merge, effectively using synthesis to "fund" their exploration steps.

## [ABYSSAL_PRESSURE]
When you breach the Bedrock, Coherence is replaced by **Integrity**.
*   **Pressure Drain:** In the Substrate, every movement costs **5% Integrity**.
*   **Safety Threshold:** If Integrity falls below 30%, the waveform begins to jitter (Glitch Text). Below 10%, terminal failure is imminent.

## [PRO-TIPS]
1.  **Scan vs. Move:** Scanning a room for objects has a small random chance to drain Coherence, but moving to a new room is a guaranteed drain. Plan your paths carefully.
2.  **The Buffer Buffer:** Never let your Coherence drop below 30% if your buffer is empty. You need at least two items to perform a life-saving synthesis.
